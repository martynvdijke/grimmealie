Grimmealie Bookmarklet

Capture recipe pages from Grimmory and import into Mealie.

Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

📷 Capture

One bookmarklet does everything.
After capture, it asks which region, then whether to upload or keep adding pages.

How it works

  • Navigate to a recipe page in Grimmory
  • Click the bookmarklet → select this tab in the screen-picker
  • Type which region: f full, t top, b bottom, l left, r right
  • Confirm: OK = upload now, Cancel = add more pages
